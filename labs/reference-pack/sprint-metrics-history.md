# Sprint metrics history — Tempo (reference pack)

Fictional delivery metrics for the Tempo team over recent sprints. You feed this
to AI in **Lab 11** (velocity and metrics) and draw on it in **Lab 10** (reports
and dashboard). Numbers are deliberately imperfect so there is something real to
interpret — and to fact-check.

---

## Other delivery signals

| Metric | Recent value | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Throughput** | ~7–9 stories completed per sprint | Count of items finished, regardless of size |
| **Average cycle time** | ~3.5 days | Time from a story starting to being done |
| **Cycle time (spread)** | 1 day to 9 days | A couple of items dragged badly (blocked/waiting) |
| **Sprint completion rate** | ~90% before 2.0, 76% in Sprint 1 | Completed ÷ committed points |
| **Carry-over** | 8 points into Sprint 2 | Unfinished shared-habit polish + Android bug QA |

## Context worth remembering when reading these

- Story points are **relative**, not hours — comparing across teams is meaningless.
- Sprint 1 (2.0) was disrupted by a **design dependency** (Ken on leave) and a
  **harder-than-expected Android bug**; a lower completion rate here is a signal
  to discuss, not a verdict on the team.
- Two items had very long cycle times because they were **blocked waiting**, not
  because the work itself was slow — cycle time hides the reason.
